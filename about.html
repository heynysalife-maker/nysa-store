Meet Nysa.

Nysa is a fashion, beauty & lifestyle influencer from India. This site is where her favourite finds live — grouped into looks and budgets so you can shop them easily. Links to retailers may be affiliate links; here’s how that works.

What this site is

Nysa Life is a curated shopping edit for India. We collect fashion, beauty, jewelry and lifestyle pieces from Indian retailers and group them into looks and budgets so they’re easier to discover.

How the finds are chosen

  • Style — pieces that work together and feel current.
  • Value — a spread of budgets, from under ₹499 upwards.
  • Availability — sold by retailers that deliver across India.

How buying works

When you tap Shop now, the retailer’s page opens in a new tab. You pay, track and return with the retailer. We never see your payment details and there’s no account to create here. Your saved items stay on your own device.

Honesty rules

  • No fabricated reviews, testimonials, likes or follower counts.
  • Prices shown are copied from retailers and may change — the retailer’s price is final.
  • Affiliate relationships are always disclosed. Read the disclosure.
