Transparency

Affiliate disclosure

In short: some links on Nysa Life are affiliate links. If you click one and buy something, we may earn a commission from the retailer. You never pay more because of it.

What an affiliate link is

An affiliate link is a tracked link to a retailer such as Myntra, Nykaa, AJIO, Amazon.in or Tata CLiQ. If a qualifying purchase happens after you click, the retailer or affiliate network pays us a small percentage. This helps keep the site free.

Which links are affiliate links

Assume that any Shop now button or product link that takes you to a retailer may be an affiliate link. These links are marked with rel="sponsored" in the page code, as recommended by search engines.

Does it affect what we show?

Finds are selected for style, price and availability. Commission rates can differ between retailers, but we don’t accept payment to feature a product, and we won’t fabricate reviews or claims.

About the picks

Finds are selected by Nysa for style, value and availability. Unless a product is clearly described as one she uses herself, a find is a recommendation, not a personal review.

Prices & availability

Prices, discounts and stock are set by retailers and can change at any time. The price on the retailer’s site at checkout is the one that applies. We can’t process orders, payments, returns or refunds — please contact the retailer for those.

Questions

If something looks wrong — a broken link or an outdated price — reach out on our social channels and we’ll fix it.
